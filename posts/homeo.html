Homeomorphic spaces have isomorphic fundamental groups

Just trying some MathJax.

Let $X$ and $Y$ be homeomorphic spaces, then their fundamental groups $\pi_1(X)$ and $\pi_1(Y)$ are isomorphic.

Define a functor $F:\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Grp}$ which sends every topological space $X$ to its fundamental group $\pi_1(X)$, and every continuous map $f:X\to Y$ to its induced homomorphism $f_*:\pi_1(X)\to\pi_1(Y)$. This preserves composition as $(fg)_*=f_*\circ g_*$ and $F(\mathrm{Id}_X)=\mathrm{Id}_{\pi_1(X)}$ so this is indeed a covariant functor. Then $F$ preserves isomorphisms. QED.
