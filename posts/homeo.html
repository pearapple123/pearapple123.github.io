Homotopically equivalent spaces have isomorphic fundamental groups

Just trying some MathJax.

Let $X$ and $Y$ be homotopically equivalent spaces, then their fundamental groups $\pi_1(X)$ and $\pi_1(Y)$ are isomorphic.

Define a functor $F:\mathbf{Top}_*\to\mathbf{Grp}$ which sends every pointed topological space $(X,x_0)$ to its fundamental group $\pi_1(X,x_0)$, and every basepoint-preserving continuous map $f:(X,x_0)\to (Y,y_0)$ to its induced homomorphism $f_*:\pi_1(X,x_0)\to\pi_1(Y,y_0)$. This preserves composition as $(fg)_*=f_*\circ g_*$ and $F(\mathrm{Id}_X)=\mathrm{Id}_{\pi_1(X)}$ so this is indeed a covariant functor.

Since $X\simeq Y$, fix basepoints $x_0\in X$ and $y_0\in Y$, then $(X,x_0)\simeq (Y,y_0)$ with homotopy equivalence $f$ and homotopic inverse $g$.
